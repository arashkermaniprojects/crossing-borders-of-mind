# Crossing the Borders of the Human Mind

A narrated, interactive companion to the review *Crossing the Borders of the Human Mind: What Learning Science, Artificial Intelligence and Neurotechnology Can, and Cannot Yet, Do for Learners*.

Open `index.html` in a browser, or visit the GitHub Pages site: https://arashkermaniprojects.github.io/crossing-borders-of-mind/

**What's inside**

- **Ten chapters:**
  - *Start here*: the question, the remainder test, and seven borders across five levels of invasiveness.
  - *The seven borders*: Intake, Attention, Forgetting, Plasticity & Time, The Skull, Transfer.
  - *Taking stock*: Where the Borders Stand, Crossing Well, and References.
- **Narration:** each chapter has a narrated tour that scrolls through and highlights its blocks. The audio was generated with Google Cloud Text-to-Speech (voice `en-US-Chirp3-HD-Charon`) and lives in `audio/`; the scripts are in `tours.json`. If the audio can't load, the browser's own voice is used instead.
- **Citations:** every in-text citation links to the original article (DOI where available), and the References chapter lists all sources.
- **Original figures:** 38 figures from open-access articles are reproduced unchanged in `img/papers/`. Each caption names its source and its Creative Commons license (CC BY, CC BY-NC or CC BY-NC-ND 4.0).
- **Animated diagrams:** all other diagrams are redrawn illustrations built as inline SVG. They animate when scrolled into view and have a replay button.

Readiness ratings and the roadmap are editorial judgements, not validated forecasts. Some illustrative diagrams (the calendar, the remainder test, the crutch and the scaffold, the plasticity pathway) are schematic; their captions say so. For exact results, refer to the original papers.
